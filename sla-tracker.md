# SLA Tracker

A practical SLA tracking framework for customer 
support teams managing high volume ticket queues 
across multiple priority levels.

## SLA Targets

| Priority | First Response | Resolution | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 24 hours | 72 hours | 48 hours |
| Medium | 8 hours | 24 hours | 16 hours |
| High | 4 hours | 8 hours | 6 hours |
| Urgent | 1 hour | 2 hours | 1.5 hours |

## SLA Breach Prevention Checklist
- Review ticket queue at start of every hour
- Flag tickets approaching SLA deadline immediately
- Escalate tickets outside your scope without delay
- Use templates and macros to reduce handle time
- Never leave a ticket without a status update
- Communicate workload issues to Team Lead early
- Prioritise high and urgent tickets above all else

## SLA Compliance Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Overall SLA Compliance | 95-100% |
| Urgent Priority Compliance | 100% |
| High Priority Compliance | 98%+ |
| Medium Priority Compliance | 95%+ |
| Low Priority Compliance | 90%+ |

## Common Reasons for SLA Breach

| Reason | Prevention |
|---|---|
| High ticket volume | Triage immediately, use templates |
| Waiting for internal team | Flag early, follow up regularly |
| Complex issues | Escalate faster, do not wait |
| Poor prioritisation | Review queue hourly |
| Inadequate documentation | Log everything in real time |
