## Ticket Management
- Acknowledge all tickets within 1 hour of receipt
- Categorise tickets by priority: Low, Medium, High, Urgent
- Assign tickets to the appropriate team or agent
- Update ticket status at every stage of resolution
- Close tickets only after customer confirmation
- Document all interactions accurately in CRM

## Ticket Priority Guide
| Priority | Example Issues | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Low | General enquiries, feedback | 24 hours |
| Medium | Account questions, billing issues | 8 hours |
| High | Service disruptions, failed transactions | 4 hours |
| Urgent | Account compromise, data issues | 1 hour |

## Escalation Matrix
| Priority | Response Time | Escalation Time | Escalated To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 24 hours | 48 hours | Senior Agent |
| Medium | 8 hours | 24 hours | Team Lead |
| High | 4 hours | 8 hours | Manager |
| Urgent | 1 hour | 2 hours | Director |

## SLA Guidelines
- **Low priority:** Resolve within 72 hours
- **Medium priority:** Resolve within 24 hours
- **High priority:** Resolve within 8 hours
- **Urgent priority:** Resolve within 2 hours
- SLA compliance target: 95-100%
- Review SLA performance weekly
- Report SLA breaches immediately to Team Lead

## Customer Communication Templates

**Opening greeting:**
> "Thank you for reaching out. My name is [Name] 
and I will be happy to assist you today."

**Acknowledgement:**
> "I understand how frustrating this must be 
and I sincerely apologise for the inconvenience 
caused."

**Requesting more information:**
> "To help resolve this as quickly as possible, 
could you please provide [specific detail]? 
I appreciate your patience."

**Resolution:**
> "I am pleased to let you know that your issue 
has been resolved. Please do not hesitate to 
reach out if you need any further assistance."

**Follow up:**
> "I am following up on your recent interaction 
with us. I want to ensure your issue was fully 
resolved and that you are satisfied with the 
outcome."

**Closing:**
> "Thank you for your patience and for choosing 
us. Have a wonderful day!"

## Handling Difficult Customers
- Always remain calm and professional regardless of tone
- Acknowledge the customer's frustration without becoming defensive
- Never interrupt — let the customer finish speaking
- Use empathy statements: "I completely understand your frustration"
- Focus on solutions not explanations
- If the situation escalates, involve a Team Lead immediately
- Document the interaction fully after resolution
- Follow up within 24 hours to confirm satisfaction

## First Contact Resolution Guide
- Understand the issue fully before responding
- Check CRM for previous interactions and history
- Provide a complete solution in the first response where possible
- Avoid transferring customers unless absolutely necessary
- Confirm resolution before closing the ticket
- FCR target: resolve at least 70% of tickets on first contact

## Agent Handover Process
- Document all open tickets with full interaction history
- Note current status, next steps and any pending actions
- Flag urgent or sensitive cases to incoming agent
- Use CRM notes to record all handover details
- Ensure no ticket is left without an assigned owner
- Confirm handover with incoming agent before signing off

## KPI Tracking
| Metric | Full Name | Target |
|---|---|---|
| CSAT | Customer Satisfaction Score | 90%+ |
| NPS | Net Promoter Score | Positive trend |
| AHT | Average Handle Time | Reduce monthly |
| FCR | First Contact Resolution | 70%+ |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement | 95-100% |

- Review KPIs weekly with Team Lead
- Identify trends and areas for improvement monthly
- Use data to inform process improvements

## Quality Standards
- Maintain professional and empathetic tone at all times
- Ensure first contact resolution where possible
- Follow up on all unresolved tickets within 24 hours
- Document all interactions accurately in CRM
- Meet or exceed CSAT and NPS targets consistently
- Adhere to company data protection and privacy policies
- Complete all mandatory training and certifications
